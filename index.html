LYSI

Email Signature Generator

Fill in your details, paste a link to your photo, then copy the signature straight into Gmail or Outlook. The LYSI logo and icons are built in — only your photo needs a hosted link.

Your details

Must be a public, direct image link ending in .jpg/.png. Square photos work best. Leave blank for a text-only signature. Google Drive share links do not work — use LinkedIn, your website, or any image host.

Preview

Install in Gmail
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Gmail → ⚙ Settings → See all settings → Signature.
  3. Create new, then paste (⌘V / Ctrl+V).
  4. Scroll down and Save Changes.
Outlook: Settings → Mail → Compose and reply → paste into the signature box. Backgrounds show as solid dark — that's expected.
